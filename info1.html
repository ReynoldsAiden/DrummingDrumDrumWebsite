What exactly are drums?

Drums are percussion instruments that are popularly used in a variety of different music genres and a variety of different events. Drums are instruments that you physically hit, whether that be with your Hands, Mallets, Sticks, or even Brushes.

What are the parts of the drumkit?

A standard drum kit is usually made up of multiple things. The snare drum produces a loud, sharp, crisp sound when hit due to metal wires being underneath the snare head which gives it a sort of rattle sound. There is also usually a kick drum present which produces that low sounding thud when striked with a kick pedal. A Hi-Hat is usually present which produces the very high, sharp sound when hit with the drum stick. Other parts include toms which are usually tuned to specific notes that are played during fils, and cymbals like a crash or a ride which are for volume and texture.
